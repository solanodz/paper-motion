DIRECTOR'S PASS (after the first full render; this is what makes it postable)
1. Make a contact sheet: 12 frames evenly spaced plus the middle of every scene,
   and a strip at 3 fps. Look at both yourself first.
2. Start a fresh subagent with no access to the code. Give it only the video
   (or the sheets) and a five-line brief: what it is, who it's for, the
   feeling, the one thing it must never look like, the format.
3. Ask it for at most 6 notes, each with a timestamp, what is wrong and a
   concrete fix, most important first, and a verdict: "ready" or "needs rework".
4. Apply the notes, highest impact first, and re-render.
5. Run a second pass only if the verdict was "needs rework". Never more than two.
6. Deliver the mp4, the final contact sheet, and one line per pass on what changed.
