# Identity

Settle **who the brand is** before anything visual: what it offers, who it is for, where people meet it, what its videos are for, its personality, its voice, and the work it admires or rejects. The result is `brand/IDENTITY.md`, the file every later skill reads first. Nothing here decides how the brand looks or moves; that is `visual-system` and `motion-style`.

## 1 · Read what exists

Read `brand/` (create it if missing).

- **`IDENTITY.md` exists:** ask what changed, re-run only those branches of the question bank, and note in the decision log that later files (visual system, motion style) may need a second look.
- **Fresh:** go to step 2.

## 2 · Pick the start

- **Import start** when the user brings material: a website, a brand manual or deck, an about page, social profiles, a doc of their own. Read all of it. Write what it **states** into the sections below, logged `By: source`. What you only **infer** or compose (a personality read from their photos, a voice read from their captions, a one-line summary assembled from their copy) becomes a recommendation to confirm, never a written decision. Then interview only what is missing: usually one or two rounds.
- **Guided start** when there is no material, or the user prefers to talk it through.

Unsure which? Ask in one line, and say that material saves them questions.

## 3 · Interview

Call the `interview` skill with the question bank below, the file `brand/IDENTITY.md`, and a depth of three to four rounds on a guided start (one or two on an import start). The bank is grouped by dependency; interview's frontier decides what actually goes in each round.

**Round 1 · what it is**
- The brand in one sentence: what it offers, and since when, for a business.
- Who it is for: the people, in their own situation ("neighbours on their way to work"), never a demographic alone.
- Where people meet it: Instagram, TikTok, a website, a shop window, a pitch deck; and so which formats its pieces need (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).
- What its videos are for: launches, everyday social posts, explaining the product, atmosphere.
- **Real material** (always asked, the user may decline): photos of the product, the place, the people or the work; screenshots or a screen recording of the product; a logo; a menu; the music. It goes to `brand/assets/` and becomes the brand's most specific material later (image filters in the shader set, footage in pieces); abstract shaders alone make every brand look alike. On an import start, ask only for what the material lacks.

**Round 2 · character**
- **Personality** as pairs, each answered with a side or "in between": calm ↔ loud, warm ↔ cool, playful ↔ serious, crafted ↔ technical, classic ↔ new, intimate ↔ grand. Recommend a position for each; the user can accept the whole set at once.
- What it is **not**: the cliché of its category it wants to avoid.
- **References**: brands, sites, films or videos they admire, and exactly what they like in each.
- **Anti-references**: work they never want to resemble, and why. When `Not` is already strong, propose anti-references that follow from it and ask only for additions.

**Round 3 · voice**
- **Voice samples**: write one real line of theirs (a greeting, a product line) three ways and let them pick A, B or C. Show the three as text in the question; no board.
- Words they use and words they never use, and the language(s) of their videos. Casing and punctuation follow from the chosen sample; ask only if it leaves them open.

**Round 4 · the sum (only if still open)**
- The brand in one line: "<brand> is <what> for <who> who <need>."
- **Three keywords** for its character. Later skills reach for these first, so they must be specific ("wood-fired, unhurried, dry humour", not "quality, passion, innovation").

## 4 · Write `brand/IDENTITY.md`

Use exactly these sections, in this order, so every later skill finds what it needs in the same place. Leave a section empty until it is asked, and `—` when the user parked it; both are listed under `## Open`. **Facts** holds only what the material or the user stated: later skills take factual copy from here and nowhere else. The file is in English, but **In one line**, **Facts** and the voice sample are written in the brand's own language, because pieces and the brand sheet put them on screen.

```md
# <Brand> · identity

## In one line
<in the brand's own language, the language of its videos>
## Offer
## Audience
## Where people meet it
## What the videos are for
## Personality
<one line per pair, a single ● marking the position: "calm ○●○○○ loud">
## Not
## Facts
<true claims later skills may use as copy: numbers, dates, places, names, license>
## Voice
<language(s), rules, then the chosen sample line>
## Words
<use: … / never: …>
## References
<one bullet each: name or link — what to take from it>
## Anti-references
## Keywords
## Open
```

Every decision also gets its entry in `brand/decisions/`, as `interview` describes.

## 5 · Close

Done when every section above is filled or explicitly parked, and the user has confirmed the interview's summary. Then show the finished `IDENTITY.md` and name the next step: `visual-system`, which turns this character into typefaces, colors and a wordmark.
