Your solar panels make the most power at noon.
But you use the most at night.
Halo stores the difference.
One: sunlight hits your panels.
Two: whatever you don't use flows into Halo, a battery the size of a suitcase.
Three: when the sun goes down, your home runs on stored sunlight.
Lower bills. Lights on, even in a blackout.
Halo. Keep the sun after dark.
