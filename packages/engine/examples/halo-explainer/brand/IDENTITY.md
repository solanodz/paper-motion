# Halo

## In one line
Halo is a home battery that stores the sun your panels make by day, so your home runs on it at night.

## Offer
A suitcase-sized home battery for houses with solar panels: it keeps the daytime surplus and powers the home after
dark and during blackouts.

## Audience
Homeowners who already have solar panels, or are about to, and want lower bills and backup power.

## Personality
Calm · warm · trustworthy · quietly confident. Intimate rather than grand; calm rather than loud.

## Voice
Plain words, short sentences, no jargon. Says what it does, then stops. Tagline: "Keep the sun after dark."
