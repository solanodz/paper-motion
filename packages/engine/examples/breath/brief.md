# Brief

Warm, quiet 4:5 loop: a breathing field of dust-colored light and a single italic word.

**Intent** — slow the viewer's breathing down for twelve seconds.
**Format** — 1080×1350 · 30fps · 12s · seamless loop · social feed / screen.
**Palette** — near-black umber ground `#1b1a17`, clay `#3d3328`, sand `#a8927a`, bone `#e9dccb`.
**Material** — MeshGradient with grain (mixer + overlay) so it reads like film, not like a UI gradient.
**Type** — one word, italic old-style serif, wide tracking; a tiny mono footnote.
**Motion** — one oscillation of scale per loop (period = duration). Letters arrive with blur→focus, leave in reverse order.
**Structure** — 0–1s field only · 1.2–3s word arrives · 4–8s hold · 8.2–10s word leaves · 10–12s field returns to frame 0.
