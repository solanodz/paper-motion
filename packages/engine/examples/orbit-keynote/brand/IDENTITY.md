# Orbit

## In one line
Orbit is a calendar for teams that schedules meetings by itself.

## Offer
You say who and how long. Orbit reads everyone's calendar, finds the gap, books the meeting, and moves it when
someone drops out, telling the rest.

## Audience
Teams of four to forty who lose hours a week to "does Tuesday work?".

## Personality
Precise · calm · quietly clever · a little playful. Cool rather than warm; new rather than classic.

## Voice
Short, direct sentences. Says what it does, with one number when it helps. Tagline: "Meetings find their own time."

## Facts
Invented brand: the presentation (product keynote) example of the paper-motion pack.

## References
- Motion: works/references/ae-style.md (the user's After Effects reference), leaned into: interface cards, chat
  bubbles, chips and toasts flying in 3D, parallax stacks, pull-back reveals, whips.
