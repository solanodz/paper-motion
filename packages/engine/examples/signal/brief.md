# Brief

Monochrome 16:9 study: a dithered sphere approaching under an instrument-like HUD.

**Intent** — the cold calm of a measurement.
**Format** — 1920×1080 · 30fps · 10s · no loop · screen / site hero.
**Palette** — carbon `#0a0a0a`, paper `#e8e6df`. Nothing else.
**Material** — Dithering (sphere, 4×4 Bayer, 3px cells): 1-bit texture, no gradients.
**Type** — mono, small, tracked, pinned to the four corners; values are driven by the frame counter.
**Motion** — a single scale gesture (in, hold, ease back). The rule fills with the clip.
