# paper-motion

## In one line
Brand videos, made by your coding agent.

## Offer
A library of motion resources with names (moves, backgrounds, palettes, fonts, recipes), a site where your agent proposes options as boards you choose from, and an engine that renders every frame.

## Not
Not an "AI magic" tool: no sparkles, no glowing gradients, no promises.

## Facts
- Open source; `npx skills add solanodz/paper-motion`.
- paper-motion.app
