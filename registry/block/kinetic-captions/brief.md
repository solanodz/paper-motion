# Kinetic captions

Block: kinetic-captions (6.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

Captions timed to a voice: the phrase sits dim, each word lights as it is said, key words land bigger, phrases change on a clean cut.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 6.2 and 6.6 s; render.
