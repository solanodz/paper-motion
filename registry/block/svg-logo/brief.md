# SVG logo

Block: svg-logo (5.4 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

The brand's own SVG logo draws on: outlines trace in the accent, shapes fill from their centre, a glint passes and the name lands beside it.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.7 and 2.4 s; render.
