# Bar grow

Block: bar-grow (5.5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

Bars grow with a short overshoot as the camera passes low along the chart; the winner takes the accent and its number counts up.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.8 and 1 s; render.
