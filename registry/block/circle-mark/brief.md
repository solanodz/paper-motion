# Circle mark

Block: circle-mark (4.5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A hand-drawn ring closes around the number that matters on a screen; the rest dims, the marked card lifts and the camera leans in close.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.9 and 1.7 s; render.
