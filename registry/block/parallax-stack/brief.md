# Parallax stack

Block: parallax-stack (5.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

An interface pulled apart into solid layers in depth; the camera orbits so each slides at its own speed, then they fall flat into one screen.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 3.4 and 4.4 s; render.
