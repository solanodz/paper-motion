# Donut fill

Block: donut-fill (5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A ring fills to its percentage as the number counts, rising from a tilted view to face-on, and a pulse rings out when it lands.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.8 and 2.8 s; render.
