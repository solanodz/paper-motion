# Callout zoom

Block: callout-zoom (5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A ring pulses on a detail too small to read; a round lens grows out of it and shows it magnified, tied back by a thin line.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.9 and 1.6 s; render.
