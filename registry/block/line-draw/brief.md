# Line draw

Block: line-draw (5.5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A trend line draws with a dot riding its tip and the camera close on it; at the peak a label pops and the camera pulls back on the climb.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.7 and 3.4 s; render.
