# Toggle flip

Block: toggle-flip (5.2 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A cursor flips a switch and the whole interface changes theme in a circle that spreads out from the switch.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.1 and 1.7999999999999998 s; render.
