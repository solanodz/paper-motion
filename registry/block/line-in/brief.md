# A sentence

Block: line-in (4 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

One sentence in the brand's display type, arriving in one of eight entrances (rise, focus, scramble, type, track,
flip, pop, bar); the marked words land in the accent and a short rule draws under it.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its sentence, the words to mark, the entrance, the alignment and the
format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.0 and 2.4 s; render. A piece may use it more than
once, each time with a different entrance.
