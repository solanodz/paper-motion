# Logo build

Block: logo-build (5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

The wordmark builds itself: each letter traces its outline, the fill rises from the baseline, and the domain lands under it.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.7 and 2.5 s; render.
