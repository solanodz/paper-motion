# Beat cuts

Block: beat-cuts (6.4 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

Every beat of the music is a hard cut to a new shot with a punch that decays; the first beat of a bar hits harder in the accent.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.4 and 6.4 s; render.
