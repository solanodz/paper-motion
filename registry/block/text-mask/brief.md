# Liquid metal word

Block: text-mask (5.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

The name in rounded, sharp-edged liquid metal; the camera travels along the letters close, then pulls back to the whole word.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 2.8 and 3.9 s; render.
