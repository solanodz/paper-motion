# Word swap

Block: word-swap (5.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

One word in a sentence keeps changing, rolling out and in, while the rest of the sentence makes room for each new width.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.8 and 4 s; render.
