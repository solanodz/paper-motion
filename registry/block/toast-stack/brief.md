# Toast stack

Block: toast-stack (5.2 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

Notifications arrive in a corner one after another and push the older ones back into a stack; the last carries the accent.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 4 and 5.2 s; render.
