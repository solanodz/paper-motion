# Grain pulse

Block: grain-pulse (4.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

On each hit the grain swells, the colour channels split for a moment and the frame punches in, then it all settles until the next.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 3.6 and 4.6 s; render.
