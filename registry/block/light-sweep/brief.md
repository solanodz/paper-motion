# Light sweep

Block: light-sweep (4.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A card turns in the light and a diagonal glint crosses its face in step with the turn, its edge catching a thin line.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 3.2 and 2.3 s; render.
