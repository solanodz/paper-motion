# Dither dissolve

Block: dither-dissolve (4.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

One shot breaks into an ordered dither, dot by dot, in a sweep across the frame; the edge lights in the accent and the next shot is under it.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.4 and 2.9 s; render.
