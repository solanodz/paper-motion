# Drag and drop

Block: drag-drop (5.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A cursor lifts a card on a board, carries it tilted to another column, a slot opens, it drops on a spring and the counts update.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.6 and 2.9 s; render.
