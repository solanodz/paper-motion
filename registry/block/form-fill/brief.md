# Form fill

Block: form-fill (7.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A cursor fills a form field by field, each check pops on, the button works and then opens into a new, black screen.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 0.7 and 2.25 s; render.
