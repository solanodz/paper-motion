# Device frame

Block: device-frame (5.5 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A phone with real thickness, the product scrolling on its screen; the camera orbits round it from the side to face-on.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1 and 4 s; render.
