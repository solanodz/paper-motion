# Browser frame

Block: browser-frame (6.4 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

The real product in a browser: the address types close up, the page loads and settles, the camera travels down it with pauses.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.3 and 2 s; render.
