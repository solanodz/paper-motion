# paper-motion · motion style

## Pace
Fast but readable: every line holds long enough to read once. Moves land on the beat, snappy in, eased out.

## Camera
One continuous camera. Places are areas of one desk; nothing cuts to black.

## Text
The terminal types itself: about 35 characters a second in bursts on the beat, a white block cursor that blinks at 2.2 Hz when idle. Agent steps slide in from −24 px over 0.25 s.

## Color
Color enters as an event ("less color."), always through a shader.

## Material
Real material only: the real session, real code, real rendered frames. No repeated frames.

## Endings
Resolve on the wordmark.
