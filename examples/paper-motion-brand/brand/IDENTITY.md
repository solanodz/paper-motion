# paper-motion · identity

## In one line
paper-motion is art-directed video for people who use coding agents, made from a conversation.

## Offer
A pack of agent skills and a render engine. It takes someone from "I have a brand" to a brand sheet and their first videos: an interview finds the identity, the look and the motion, and the engine renders frame-exact video with Paper Shaders.

## Audience
Founders, developers and small studios who already work with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and want videos with taste, without learning After Effects. Designers who know their brand and want to skip to the making.

## Where people meet it
GitHub (the README loop), X and LinkedIn (16:9 and 1:1 clips), the terminal (`npx skills add solanodz/paper-motion`). Formats: 16:9 first, 1:1 for feeds.

## What the videos are for
Showing the skill at work: real sessions, real code, real renders. Launches of new versions, and the README loop.

## Not
Not an "AI magic" tool: no sparkles, no glowing gradients, no promises. Not a template machine: every piece starts from the user's intent.

## Facts
- Open source; skills installable with `npx skills add solanodz/paper-motion`.
- Renders with Paper Shaders (paper.design), Apache 2.0.
- Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and other agents that read skills.
