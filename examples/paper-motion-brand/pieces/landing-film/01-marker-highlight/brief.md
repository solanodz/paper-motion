# Marker highlight

Block: marker-highlight (4.8 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

A sentence lands word by word, then a tilted marker sweeps behind the key words, which turn dark exactly where it passes.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 1.2 and 2.3 s; render.
