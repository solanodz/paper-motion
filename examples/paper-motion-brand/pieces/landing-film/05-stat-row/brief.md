# Stat row

Block: stat-row (5.6 s), from paper-motion.app. The look, fonts and sound come from `brand/`.

Three numbers count up in turn with rules drawing between them; the camera meets each as it counts, then pulls back to all three.

A block is one ready-made moment. Edit `CONFIG` (its words, numbers and format: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, the layout recomposes for each); check `npx paper-motion still` at 2.9 and 4.6 s; render.
